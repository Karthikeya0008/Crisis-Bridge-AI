Problem statement

Signal lost in the noise

During floods, cyclones and earthquakes, thousands of posts arrive within minutes. Trapped people, urgent medical needs and precise locations are buried among rumour, sympathy messages and irrelevant content. Prior systems such as AIDR and TREC-IS classify the stream, but a coordinator still cannot simply ask, “What is critical right now, near XYZ?”

0.36%of disaster tweets in India carry precise geo-tags, so location must be inferred from text
77K+human-labelled crisis tweets across 19 real disaster events (HumAID)

What happens
  1. 1
    Humanitarian classification

    TF-IDF + logistic regression over HumAID's labels filters informative reports from noise.

  2. 2
    NER + urgency detection

    Extracts location, need type and people count, then scores urgency on an explainable 0–100 scale.

  3. 3
    Into the RAG index

    Optionally embedded and added, so commander queries can retrieve and cite it straight away.
